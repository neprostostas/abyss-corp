# Immersive 3D direction — iteration 1

The scene fills the entire viewport. There is no page header, footer, bottom upgrade deck or fixed stage rectangle. Retain Russo One display lettering, Manrope explanatory copy, ivory/gold actions, turquoise status light, softly rounded dark navy equipment housings and the Abyss brand.

## Interaction rules implemented

- A compact wallet and brand float in corners; no full-width interface bars.
- Depth/location is an instrument capsule, with outpost navigation on demand.
- Clicking a real model selects it. Its world-space bounds project to screen space; the equipment card follows that anchor while orbiting. Blank-water click and Escape dismiss it.
- A single selected machine exposes cycle state, current/next performance, actual upgrade cost and affordability. Stock and sale are available at the depot. No permanent four-card wall.
- On narrow screens the contextual card becomes a bottom sheet rather than chasing a small moving model.
- Research, missions/expeditions and workshop are on-demand service drawers. They retain tested economic rules and existing content, with independent placement outside the old page grid.
- A small keyboard-accessible equipment selector supplements direct object picking. Camera reset/help and touch direction controls remain available.
- Submarine travel follows production phase; rotor slows while waiting. Bubbles and a small geometric 3D fish school add environmental motion.
